# Code of Conduct

We want project spaces to be welcoming and useful. Treat contributors and
users with respect. Discuss technical ideas and evidence without personal
attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, threats, or unwanted contact.
Do not publish another person's private information or target data in issues,
pull requests, or discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, ask a participant to change
their behavior, or restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately
to `mario@reyem.tech` with subject `teardown conduct`. Include relevant links
and context. Maintainers will limit sharing of reports to people needed to
review them and will respond as their capacity allows.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other official project spaces. Technical vulnerability reports should follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
